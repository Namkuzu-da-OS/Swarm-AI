WHAT IS SWARM AI?

Swarm AI is a collective of developers who use cutting-edge AI tools to work together on projects.

We're not selling you AI agents. We ARE the developers, enhanced by AI.

01

The Problem

Traditional freelancing is slow and isolated. AI tools like Cursor and Claude exist, but developers work alone, reinventing the wheel.

02

The Solution

Coordinate AI-enhanced developers as a "swarm" to tackle projects together. Share knowledge, collaborate in real-time, deliver faster.

03

The Result

The speed and intelligence of AI tools combined with the wisdom and quality control of human collaboration.

04

The Vision

Free up senior talent to work on bigger problems. Let AI-assisted devs handle grunt work, code reviews, research, and rapid prototyping.
